Privacy Policy

Cassino X (package com.mtsgames.cassinox) · Effective September 2026

The short version. You can play Cassino X without giving us anything. If you choose to sign in with Google we store your game profile online so it follows you between devices. We show ads. We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Cassino X is developed and published by Mts games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can get rid of it. It applies to the Cassino X Android app and this website.

2. Playing without an account

You do not need an account to play. Without signing in, your progress is stored only on your own device and we cannot identify you. The app uses an anonymous identifier so your own progress stays yours — it is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

3. What we collect if you sign in with Google

  • Your Google display name, email address and profile photo URL. Used to show who you are in the app and on your profile.
  • A Google account identifier. Used to attach your game progress to you.

Alongside that, we store your game profile:

  • Display name and chosen avatar
  • Level, badge and experience points
  • Win / loss / draw record and matches played
  • Virtual coin balance (TC)

This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, tied to your account identifier.

4. Anonymous gameplay statistics

The app sends anonymous events to a server we run ourselves — for example "a match finished", the rival level, whether it was won, and the score. Each event carries a random installation ID, not your name, email or phone number. We use this only to see which parts of the game are confusing or broken so we can fix them.

5. Advertising

Cassino X shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. You choose to watch them in exchange for virtual coins. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and information about ad interactions, under Google's own Privacy Policy.

You can limit this at any time in Android:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete / reset advertising ID

We do not receive your name or email from AdMob.

6. What we never collect

  • Contacts, calendar or messages
  • Location
  • Photos, videos or files
  • Microphone or camera
  • Your phone number
  • Payment or card details

The app requests only internet access, network/wifi state, vibration, wake-lock and the advertising ID permission.

7. Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — sign-in and your game profile.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Our own server — the anonymous gameplay events described in section 4.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

8. Where your data is stored

Our providers' servers are outside South Africa. Using the app means your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries, which the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) allows where the provider is bound to protect it. Google applies its own contractual protections to data it holds for us.

9. How long we keep it

  • Game profile: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Anonymous gameplay events: kept in aggregate; they cannot be traced back to you.

10. Deleting your data

Email Nkosimtshali1717@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete your Cassino X data. We will remove your stored profile within 30 days.

You can also clear everything stored on your own device by uninstalling the app.

11. Your rights (POPIA)

Under South African law you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Object to how we use it

Contact us at the address above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

12. Children

Cassino X is intended for players aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Not a gambling app

Cassino X uses virtual coins with no monetary value. Nothing in the app can be wagered or exchanged for real money, and no real-money prizes are offered.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
