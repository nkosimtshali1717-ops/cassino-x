How to play SA Cassino

  • Chow — take the table cards that match your card. Aces and tens also sweep the whole table.
  • Build — set a value on the table that you must collect on a later turn.
  • Drift — add your card to the table and claim nothing.
  • Score — most cards, most spades, and the ten of diamonds. First to 21 points wins.
