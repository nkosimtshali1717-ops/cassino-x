CASSINO X

The 40-card game South Africa grew up playing.

What it is

  • 40 cards, no face cards. The SA rules, played straight.
  • Capture, build, steal. Tap CHOW, BUILD or DRIFT — no dragging.
  • Climb 15 levels from Noob to Grand Master.
  • Play with your people. Local multiplayer on one phone, or online.
  • Free. Coins are earned by playing and watching ads. They cannot be bought with cash and cannot be cashed out.

Not a gambling app

  • No real money is staked, wagered or won. Ever.
